Cancellation by Athlete

  • Registration fees are non-refundable once payment is successfully completed, except as described below.
  • If a cancellation request is made at least 7 days before the event (i.e., on or before 2 October 2025), 50% of the registration fee may be refunded at the organizers' discretion.
  • No refunds will be processed for cancellation requests made within 7 days of the event.
  • No-shows on the day of the event are not eligible for any refund.

Cancellation or Postponement by Organizers

In the rare event that NPC Regionals Chandigarh 2026 is cancelled or postponed by the organizers due to unforeseen circumstances, registered athletes will be offered either a full refund or the option to transfer their registration to the rescheduled date, at the organizers' discretion.

Duplicate or Failed Payments

If you are charged more than once for the same registration, or a payment fails but an amount is deducted from your account, please contact us with your payment ID/transaction reference. Verified duplicate or failed-but-deducted payments will be refunded in full within 7–10 business days to the original payment method, in line with Razorpay's settlement timelines.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at info@npcregionalchandigarh.com or call +91 98765 43210 with your full name, registration category, and payment ID. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
