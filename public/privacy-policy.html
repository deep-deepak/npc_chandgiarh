Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NPC Regionals Chandigarh 2026 ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects the personal information of athletes and visitors who register for or interact with our event through this website.

Information We Collect

When you register for the event, we collect the following information through our registration form:

  • Full name, email address, phone number, and emergency contact number
  • Gender and date of birth
  • City of residence
  • Event category, entry type, and weight category/division
  • Any additional message you choose to share with us

Payment Information

Registration fee payments are processed securely through Razorpay, our third-party payment gateway partner. We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers. We only retain the payment ID, order ID, amount paid, and payment status for record-keeping and verification purposes. Please refer to Razorpay's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your event registration
  • To verify eligibility for your selected category and entry type
  • To communicate important event updates, schedules, and instructions
  • To contact you or your emergency contact in case of a medical or safety concern during the event

Data Storage

Registration data submitted through this website is stored in a secure Google Sheet accessible only to the event organizing team. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party other than our payment processor (Razorpay) for the purpose of completing your transaction.

Data Retention

We retain registration records for a reasonable period after the event for record-keeping, dispute resolution, and future event communication purposes, unless you request deletion earlier.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us at info@npcregionalchandigarh.com or call +91 98765 43210.
